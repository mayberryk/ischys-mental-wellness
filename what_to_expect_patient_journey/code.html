Your Path to Healing

Understanding what to expect can ease the anxiety of starting psychiatric care. We believe in a transparent, thoughtful process tailored to your unique needs.

Step 01

The Preparation

  • calendar_month

    Schedule Consultation

    Begin by finding a time that works for you through our secure scheduling portal.

  • description

    Complete Paperwork

    Fill out necessary forms in our secure portal prior to your visit, allowing us to focus entirely on you during our time together.

  • prescriptions

    Prepare Information

    Gather your current medication list, prior medical records, and review any insurance or self-pay details.

Step 02

The Conversation

  • timer

    90-Minute Evaluation

    Generous time to tell your story, share your symptoms, and review your medical history without feeling rushed.

  • psychology

    Whole-Person Review

    Conducting a comprehensive assessment of sleep, stress, trauma, nutrition, hormones, lifestyle, and your care goals.

  • forum

    Open & Unhurried Space

    Every question is welcomed and answered thoroughly in a warm, collaborative, and safe environment.

Step 03

The Plan

  • clinical_notes

    Diagnostic & Treatment Clarity

    Clear explanation of findings, diagnostic insights, and reviewing all evidence-based options together.

  • medication

    Medication & Holistic Options

    Thorough discussion of medication options (if indicated), minimal effective dosing strategies, and non-medication supports.

  • checklist

    Collaborative Action Steps

    Establishing a personalized, step-by-step roadmap tailored to your lifestyle, preferences, and long-term goals.

Step 04

Ongoing Care

  • update

    Medication & Care Optimization

    Continuous monitoring of symptoms, side effects, and fine-tuning medication levels over time.

  • groups

    Care Team Coordination

    Active, ongoing communication with your therapist, PCP, and other providers for a seamless care experience.

  • school

    Patient Empowerment & Education

    Equipping you with mental health education, self-advocacy tools, and long-term wellness strategies.
