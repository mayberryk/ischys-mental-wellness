My Story & Mission

Why I Dedicated My Life to Mental Healthcare

favorite

30+ Years of Lifelong Compassion

My career has taken me from hospital rooms to veterans’ clinics, from community health programs to private practice, but my heart has always stayed the same: I am here to help people find hope and strength, even when life feels overwhelming.

diversity_3

Advocating for Overlooked Communities

I specialize in mental health and addiction care, serving veterans, families, individuals with autism, and the LGBTQ+ community. These groups hold a special place in my heart because I know how often they are overlooked, dismissed, or told to "just deal with it." My approach combines evidence-based medicine with natural, whole-person care—restoring balance, resilience, and joy.

psychology_alt

Empathy Born of Personal Experience

I know what it feels like to struggle. My own journey hasn’t been easy—navigating grief, setbacks, and obstacles in both my personal life and my career. But those experiences shaped me into the clinician I am today. They taught me empathy, grit, and the belief that no one is ever broken beyond repair.

holiday_village

Creating Sanctuaries & Expanding Access

When I dreamed of building my own clinics, I imagined spaces that felt less like doctor’s offices and more like sanctuaries—welcoming, calm, and stigma-free. And because I believe access matters, I am passionate about bringing these resources not only to Utah cities, but also to rural Utah where care is scarce.

school

Shaping the Future of Mental Health Care

I know how difficult it is for students to find clinical placements—it was one of the hardest parts of my own training. That’s why I’m building a practice that also functions as a training hub for future therapists and Psychiatric-Mental Health Nurse Practitioners. Supporting the next generation is one of the best ways I can give back to the profession that has given me so much.

Why work with a Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner?

A PMHNP brings a unique, nursing-based perspective to psychiatric care. This means a fundamental commitment to treating the whole person—mind, body, and spirit—rather than just addressing a list of symptoms.

We place a strong emphasis on patient education, ensuring you understand your condition and your options. The relationship is inherently collaborative; we work together to find the path forward. At the core of this approach is the therapeutic bond, built on trust, empathy, and an unhurried presence.
