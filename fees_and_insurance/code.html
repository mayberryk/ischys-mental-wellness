Understanding Insurance Terms

payments

Deductible

The amount you pay out-of-pocket for covered healthcare services before your insurance plan starts to pay. If your deductible is unmet, you will be responsible for our full contracted rate until it is reached.

receipt_long

Copay / Coinsurance

A fixed amount (copay) or a percentage of the cost (coinsurance) you pay for a covered healthcare service after you've paid your deductible. This is typically due at the time of service.

fact_check

Prior Authorization

A decision by your health insurer or plan that a specific service or medication is medically necessary. Sometimes required before treatment can begin or certain medications can be prescribed.

travel_explore

Out-of-Network

If we do not accept your insurance, you may still have "out-of-network" benefits. You pay our private pay rate upfront, and we provide a document you can submit to your insurance for potential partial reimbursement.

description

Superbill

An itemized receipt detailing the services provided, diagnosis codes, and fees paid. Patients provide superbills to their insurance companies to claim out-of-network benefits.

account_balance_wallet

Patient Responsibility

The portion of a medical bill that the patient is required to pay, rather than the insurance company. This includes copays, coinsurance, and any unmet deductibles.

verified_user Good Faith Estimate

Under the No Surprises Act, health care providers need to give patients who don't have insurance or who are not using insurance an estimate of the bill for medical items and services.

You have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate for the total expected cost of any non-emergency items or services. We will provide this estimate in writing at least 1 business day before your scheduled medical service.

Frequently Asked Billing Questions

We are in-network with several major carriers as listed above. However, plans vary widely even within the same carrier (e.g., HMO vs. PPO, state-specific plans). The best way to know for sure is to provide us with your insurance information during intake, or contact your carrier directly to ask if "Ischys Mental Wellness" is an in-network provider.

If you have a deductible that has not been met for the year, you will be responsible for the full contracted rate for our services until that deductible amount is reached. Once met, you will only be responsible for your copay or coinsurance amount.

If you are seeing us out-of-network, we can provide a superbill upon request at the end of each month. You can usually submit this document through your insurance company's online portal or by mailing it to the claims department address listed on the back of your insurance card. Reimbursement rates depend entirely on your specific out-of-network benefits.

While we do our best to verify benefits beforehand, insurance verification is not a guarantee of payment. If a claim is denied, you will be responsible for the balance. We will, of course, assist in providing any necessary documentation if an appeal is appropriate.
