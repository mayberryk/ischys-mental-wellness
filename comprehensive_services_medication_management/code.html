Why Choose a PMHNP?

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our psychiatric approach.

Why choose a Psychiatric Nurse Practitioner (PMHNP) over a psychiatrist? expand_more
A Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner brings a rich 30+ year nursing foundation that emphasizes treating the whole person—mind, body, lifestyle, and spirit—rather than spending 15 minutes checking off symptoms. You receive comprehensive psychiatric evaluation and full prescribing authority paired with a compassionate, unhurried model of care.
What is Alpha-Stim therapy and how does it help? expand_more
Alpha-Stim is an FDA-cleared, non-invasive cranial electrotherapy stimulation (CES) device. It passes gentle, patented microcurrents to regulate brain wave activity, offering clinically proven relief from anxiety, insomnia, and mood dysregulation without pharmaceutical side effects.
Will I need medication forever? expand_more
Our goal is always to find the minimum effective dose for the shortest necessary duration. We will continuously evaluate your progress and discuss long-term strategies.
What if I am afraid to start medication? expand_more
It's completely normal to have concerns. We will discuss all your fears openly, explain the science, and ensure you feel comfortable before making any decisions.
Can I stop my antidepressant? expand_more
Stopping medication requires careful planning and a gradual taper to avoid withdrawal symptoms. We will guide you safely through this process when appropriate.
